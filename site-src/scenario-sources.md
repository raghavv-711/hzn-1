# Sources for the "what if" scenarios (researched 2026-10-07)

Base factors
- 3.16 kg CO2 per kg Jet A, tank-to-wake (ICAO Carbon Calculator methodology): https://www.icao.int/sites/default/files/sp-files/environmental-protection/CarbonOffset/Documents/Methodology%20ICAO%20Carbon%20Calculator_v8-2015.pdf
- Fossil jet lifecycle baseline 89 gCO2e/MJ (CORSIA); ≈3.84 kgCO2e/kg well-to-wake at 43.2 MJ/kg: https://www.icao.int/sites/default/files/sp-files/environmental-protection/CORSIA/Documents/CORSIA%20Leaflets/CorsiaLeaflet-EN-9-WEB.pdf

Sustainable aviation fuel
- CORSIA default lifecycle values (Nov 2021): HEFA used cooking oil 13.9 g/MJ (−84%), HEFA tallow 22.5 (−75%), HEFA soybean 64.9 (−27%), FT ag residues 7.7 (−91%), FT municipal waste 5.2 (−94%), alcohol-to-jet ag residues 29.3 (−67%): https://www.icao.int/sites/default/files/sp-files/environmental-protection/CORSIA/Documents/ICAO%20document%2006%20-%20Default%20Life%20Cycle%20Emissions%20-%20November%202021.pdf
- Max certified blend 50% (ASTM D7566); 100% SAF not yet certified: https://easa.europa.eu/en/domains/environment/eaer/sustainable-aviation-fuels/what-are-sustainable-aviation-fuels
- Global SAF output 2025: 1.9 Mt, 0.6% of jet fuel (IATA, Dec 2025): https://www.iata.org/en/pressroom/2025-releases/2025-12-09-04/
- ReFuelEU mandate: 2% (2025), 6% (2030), 20% (2035), 34% (2040), 42% (2045), 70% (2050): https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/2405/oj

Battery-electric
- ~200 Wh/kg aviation-certifiable packs today (H55): https://h55.ch/technology/
- ICCT: 250 Wh/kg "current", Li-ion/solid-state likely ≤400–500 Wh/kg; electric cuts CO2e 49–88% depending on the grid: https://theicct.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/global-aviation-performance-analysis-regional-electric-aircraft-jul22-1.pdf-1.pdf

Hybrid-electric regional
- Heart Aerospace ES-36 (replaced ES-30, Sep 2026): 36 seats, 200 km electric, 1,200 km hybrid, service 2031: https://avweb.com/aviation-news/heart-unveils-es-36-with-jsx-order/
- RTX hybrid Dash 8 demonstrator: up to 30% fuel/CO2 reduction (target): https://www.rtx.com/news/2026/03/03/rtxs-hybrid-electric-plane-is-one-step-closer-to-the-sky

Hydrogen
- Airbus ZEROe postponed (Feb–Mar 2025), concept ≥100 seats, ~1,000 nm, fuel cells: https://airbus.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-03-airbus-showcases-hydrogen-aircraft-technologies-during-its-2025
- H2 120 MJ/kg but LH2 ~8 MJ/L vs Jet A ~34.7 MJ/L (≈4× the volume): https://www.energy.gov/eere/fuelcells/hydrogen-storage
- Hydrogen production: grey 10–12 kgCO2e/kg H2, green ~0.4–2.7 (IEA Global Hydrogen Review 2024): https://www.iea.org/reports/global-hydrogen-review-2024/ghg-emissions-of-hydrogen-and-its-derivatives

All aircraft figures are pre-certification targets; non-CO2 effects (contrails) are excluded.
